## Where the weight is, against where the chapters are

| Section | Chapters | Exam weight |
|---|---:|---:|
| 1 Fundamentals of gen AI | 6 | 30% |
| 2 Google Cloud’s gen AI offerings | 6 | 35% |
| 3 Techniques to improve gen AI model output | 5 | 20% |
| 4 Business strategies for a successful gen AI solution | 4 | 15% |

Sections 3 and 4 carry 35% of the exam and more than a third of the
chapters. That is deliberate. The product documentation behind this
exam covers sections 1 and 2 well and barely touches the judgement
half, so those are the chapters a reader has nowhere else to get.
